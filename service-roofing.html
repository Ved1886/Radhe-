ROOFING & CLADDING

Weather-Tight Industrial Roofing Solutions

At Radhe Infrastructure, we engineer and install comprehensive industrial roofing and architectural wall cladding envelopes designed to withstand severe thermal expansion, high cyclonic wind uplift forces, and aggressive chemical environments. Our roofing systems safeguard multi-crore industrial assets, machinery, and inventory across Gujarat and Western India.

We deploy prime-grade 550 MPa high-tensile Galvalume (Al-Zn alloy coated steel) and insulated composite panels engineered with anti-capillary drainage grooves, sealed side laps, and specialized EPDM fastening systems. Every installation is meticulously calculated for structural wind loads per IS 875 (Part 3), ensuring complete thermal efficiency, acoustic insulation, and zero water ingress even under intense monsoon downpours.

  • Single & Double Skin Systems: Economical single-layer trapezoidal cladding or high-performance double skin insulated build-ups with sub-girts, vapor barriers, and rockwool/glasswool infill.
  • Insulated Sandwich Panels (PUF/Rockwool): Continuous polyurethane foam and high-density mineral wool panels providing extraordinary thermal U-values (down to 0.20 W/m²K) and certified fire containment.
  • Color-Coated Galvalume Sheets: AZ150 zinc-aluminum alloy coating with Regular Modified Polyester (RMP), Siliconized Polyester (SMP), or PVDF coatings resistant to UV degradation and acid rain.
  • Standing Seam & Crimp-Curved Roofing: 360° mechanically seamed profiles with concealed sliding clips eliminating screw penetrations, along with custom radius curved eaves for aesthetic architectural envelopes.
  • 25-Year Coating Warranty: Certified performance warranties against corrosion perforation, blistering, cracking, and premature color fade in harsh coastal and industrial environments.

ENGINEERED CATALOG

Our Industrial Roofing & Cladding Product Range

Manufactured with high-precision roll-forming technology, state-of-the-art polyurethane injection lines, and rigorous quality inspection protocols matching international ASTM and BIS benchmarks.

Trapezoidal Profile Sheets

Deep 30mm–35mm crest profiles with subtle ribbing for enhanced moment of inertia and heavy rainfall discharge. Available in 0.47mm to 0.80mm TCT bare Galvalume (AZ150) and pre-painted steel (PPGL) in multiple vibrant RAL shades.

Sandwich Panels (PUF Core)

Pre-insulated composite panels with 40±2 kg/m³ CFC/HCFC-free rigid polyurethane foam core. Delivers exceptional thermal conductivity (λ = 0.024 W/m·K), tongue-and-groove joint airtightness, and up to 40% HVAC energy savings.

Rockwool Insulated Panels

Non-combustible Class A-1 fire-rated mineral fiber core panels providing up to 120 minutes of certified fire barrier protection and high sound transmission loss (STC ≥ 32 dB) for chemical, refinery, and acoustic environments.

Standing Seam Roofing

Zero-penetration continuous length roofing seamed on-site with 360-degree mechanical lock seams. Fastened via floating concealed thermal expansion clips, virtually eliminating puncture risk on extremely low slope roofs down to 1.5°.

Polycarbonate Skylights

Multi-wall and profiled UV-coextruded polycarbonate daylight sheets engineered to match trapezoidal sheet corrugations precisely. Transmits up to 80% diffused natural light while filtering 99.9% of harmful ultraviolet radiation.

Ridge Caps & Flashing

CNC press-brake fabricated corner flashings, vented ridge caps, drip trims, curved eaves, barge boards, and heavy-duty 1.6mm galvanized valley gutters engineered to create a fully continuous, water-tight perimeter building envelope.

ENERGY-EFFICIENT ENVELOPES

Solar-Ready Framing & Natural Airflow Ventilation

Modern industrial facilities demand more than just weather protection—they require energy self-sufficiency and superior passive climate regulation. Radhe Infrastructure designs and installs high-performance roofing envelopes engineered specifically for rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) arrays and continuous gravity-driven ventilation.

Our solar-ready structural roofs incorporate reinforced purlin sizing and zero-puncture standing seam clamp fixtures, eliminating water leakage while supporting heavy solar panel dead loads and cyclonic wind uplifts. Paired with aerodynamic ridge ventilators and daylighting polycarbonate panels, our systems cut indoor temperature by up to 8°C and reduce artificial lighting energy by up to 60%.

  • Non-Penetrative Solar Clamping: Standing seam clamps certified for high-wind resistance without piercing outer roof membranes.
  • Aerodynamic Ridge & Turbo Ventilators: Continuous natural airflow exhaust expelling trapped heat, industrial humidity, and fumes.
  • Multi-Layer Thermal Barrier: Composite insulation ensuring optimal working temperature for factory personnel and sensitive stored cargo.
