PEB STRUCTURES

Engineered for Speed, Strength & Economy

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) represent the modern standard of structural engineering, combining unmatched architectural flexibility with rapid execution. Our PEB solutions are custom-engineered to meet precise client operational requirements, ensuring optimal steel utilization without compromising structural integrity.

By leveraging advanced Tekla 3D BIM modeling and state-of-the-art CNC automated manufacturing, we deliver structures that achieve up to 30% faster installation and 20% cost savings compared to conventional structural steel. Furthermore, our PEBs are highly durable, earthquake resistant, and utilize 100% recyclable high-yield structural steel.

  • Clear-Span up to 90m: Uninterrupted interior floor space without intermediate columns for maximum operational and storage efficiency.
  • Design Life 50+ Years: Fabricated with high-grade E350 steel and multi-coat anti-corrosive industrial epoxy/polyurethane finishes.
  • Seismic Zone V & Wind Compliant: Rigorously engineered to withstand severe cyclonic wind speeds (up to 44–50 m/s) and seismic forces per IS 1893.
  • Integrated Overhead Crane Girders: Designed to accommodate heavy-duty EOT cranes up to 50MT capacity with vibration-dampened runway beams.
  • Energy Efficient Envelopes: High-performance standing seam or insulated PUF sandwich panels for optimal thermal barrier and zero leakage.

STRUCTURAL COMPONENTS

Anatomy of a PEB Structure

Understanding the meticulously engineered elements that make up our high-performance Pre-Engineered Buildings.

Primary Rigid Frames

Solid-web tapered portal columns and rafter sections engineered per IS 800:2007 with high-yield E350 steel, maximizing clear-span operational area.

Secondary Framework

High-strength cold-formed galvanized Z & C purlins, eave struts, and sag rods providing robust aerodynamic secondary support and bracing.

Roof & Cladding Systems

High-performance standing seam or insulated PUF/Rockwool sandwich panels delivering weatherproof sealing, thermal barrier, and durability.

Crane Runway System

Heavy-duty EOT crane runway beams, bracket assemblies, and gantry girders supporting up to 50MT lifting operations with zero vibration.

Mezzanine Floor Systems

Heavy-duty steel mezzanine platforms with composite deck slabs, handrails, and industrial access staircases for multi-level floor utility.

Ventilation & Daylighting

Wind-driven turbo ventilators, continuous ridge vents, and UV-coated polycarbonate skylight panels ensuring energy-efficient daylighting.
