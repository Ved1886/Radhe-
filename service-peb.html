Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB)

PEB STRUCTURES

Engineered for Speed, Strength & Economy

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) represent the modern era of structural engineering, combining exceptional design flexibility with rapid execution. Our PEB solutions are custom-engineered to meet precise client requirements, ensuring optimal steel utilization without compromising structural integrity.

By leveraging advanced design software and state-of-the-art manufacturing, we deliver structures that offer up to 30% faster construction and 20% cost savings compared to conventional steel buildings. Furthermore, our PEBs are highly durable, earthquake resistant, and utilize 100% recyclable steel.

  • Clear-Span up to 90m: Uninterrupted floor space for maximum operational efficiency.
  • Design Life 50+ Years: Built with high-grade steel and superior anti-corrosion treatments.
  • Seismic Zone V Compliant: Engineered to withstand severe seismic and wind loads.
  • Fire Resistant Options: Available with intumescent coatings for enhanced fire safety.
  • Low Maintenance: Superior finishes require minimal upkeep over the building's lifecycle.
STRUCTURAL COMPONENTS

Anatomy of a PEB Structure

Understanding the meticulously engineered elements that make up our high-performance buildings.

Primary Frames

Rigid frames, tapered columns & rafters engineered per stringent IS 800:2007 structural standards for optimal load distribution.

Purlins & Girts

High-strength hot-rolled & cold-formed Z/C sections forming the secondary structural framework for roof and wall support.

Roof Panels

Thermally efficient insulated sandwich panels or durable single-skin color-coated sheets ensuring complete weather protection.

Wall Cladding

Aesthetically pleasing pre-painted galvalume sheets backed by an industry-leading 25-year coating warranty.

Crane Beams

Heavy-duty EOT crane beams supporting up to 20T capacity, featuring meticulously designed runway systems for smooth operations.

Mezzanine Floors

Robust steel mezzanine floor systems seamlessly integrated to provide additional, high-load bearing usable floor area.

Turbo Ventilators

Eco-friendly, roof-mounted wind-driven ventilators engineered to ensure continuous natural air circulation and exhaust.

Skylights & Accessories

High-impact polycarbonate skylights, precision-engineered ridge ventilators, and architectural louvers for natural lighting.
