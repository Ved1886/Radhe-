CIVIL WORKS

Strong Foundations for Lasting Structures

At Radhe Infrastructure, we recognize that the enduring stability and structural longevity of every industrial asset depends fundamentally on the integrity of its substructure. We provide comprehensive, turnkey civil engineering and industrial foundation contracting tailored explicitly to interface seamlessly with Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEB), heavy process machinery, automated warehousing, and factory complexes across Gujarat and Western India.

Our civil division combines rigorous geotechnical soil investigation (SBC assessment, exploratory borehole logging, plate load testing) with advanced structural RCC design conforming strictly to IS 456:2000 standards. From earth excavation and soil stabilization to precision shuttering, Fe-550D TMT reinforcement fabrication, high-grade ready-mix concrete pouring, and laser-guided screeding, we guarantee that anchor bolts and column base plates achieve micrometer-level alignment tolerances for seamless structural erection.

  • RCC Isolated & Combined Footings: Custom engineered deep and shallow footing configurations optimized for high axial compression, shear, and uplift moment loads induced by wind and seismic forces.
  • Pedestal & Plinth Beam Construction: Heavy-duty reinforced tie-beams and stub columns cast with laser-aligned anchor bolt clusters for millimetric PEB column anchoring.
  • Industrial Flooring (VDF/Tremix): High abrasion-resistant Vacuum Dewatered Concrete (VDF) floors featuring non-metallic quartz hardeners, polypropylene crack-control fibers, and sealed expansion joints.
  • Retaining Walls & Compound Walls: Cast-in-situ reinforced concrete retaining structures, flood prevention containment, and heavy-duty precast or masonry boundary perimeters.
  • Underground Utility Trenches: Interconnected cable raceways, reinforced effluent piping trenches, sumps, pump foundations, and rainwater harvesting recharge pits.

ENGINEERING LIFECYCLE

Scope of Work & Engineering Stages

From initial subsoil geotechnical characterization to ready-to-erect handover, our turnkey civil construction methodology ensures zero settlement and absolute safety.

Site Survey & Soil Testing

Digital Total Station topographical surveys, contour mapping, exploratory borehole drilling, Standard Penetration Tests (SPT), Safe Bearing Capacity (SBC) determination, and subsoil chemical analysis for sulfate and chloride resistance.

Foundation Design (IS 456)

Rigorous structural calculations using STAAD.Pro conforming strictly to IS 456:2000 (RCC), IS 1893:2016 (Earthquake Resistance), and IS 875 (Wind Loads). Complete Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) and anchor bolt embedment templates.

RCC Construction

Mechanized earth excavation, anti-termite chemical treatment, 100mm PCC mud mat, Fe-550D TMT reinforcement cages, heavy-duty steel formwork, and mechanized pouring of M25/M30/M40 RMC with vibratory compaction and curing.

Industrial Floor Slabs

Heavy-duty ground-supported slabs built with Vacuum Dewatered Flooring (VDF/Tremix) technology. Monolithic power-trowel finish with mineral hardeners for FM2 surface classification and polysulphide saw-cut joint sealing.

Drainage & Utilities

Subterranean RCC stormwater channels, box culverts, internal cable trenches with chequered MS covers, fire hydrant piping beds, heavy machine isolation vibration dampers, and chemical effluent sumps.

Finishing & Handover

Total Station coordinate validation of column centerlines (+/- 1.5mm tolerance), anchor bolt projection verification, cube compressive strength test reports, plinth compaction, and certified turnkey handover for PEB steel erection.
