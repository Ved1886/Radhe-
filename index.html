OUR PROCESS

How We Deliver Excellence

STEP 01

Consultation & Design

Requirement analysis, site survey, and 3D structural design using Tekla & STAAD.Pro.

STEP 02

Precision Fabrication

CNC cutting, SAW welding, shot blasting, and anti-corrosive painting at our facility.

STEP 03

Logistics & Site Erection

Planned dispatch, crane erection, bolt torquing, and structural alignment on site.

STEP 04

Handover & Quality Sign-Off

Final inspection, dimensional verification, snag list clearance, and project handover.
